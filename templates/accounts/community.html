Community Guidelines

Last Updated: July 2026

VideoSnap is committed to creating a safe, respectful, and enjoyable environment for everyone. By using our platform, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines.

1. Be Respectful

  • Treat all users with kindness and respect.
  • Avoid harassment, bullying, or hate speech.
  • Respect different opinions and cultures.

2. Share Appropriate Content

  • Upload only content that you own or have permission to use.
  • Do not upload violent, abusive, or illegal content.
  • Avoid misleading or deceptive videos.

3. Protect Privacy

  • Do not share personal or confidential information.
  • Respect the privacy of others.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organization.

4. No Spam

  • Do not post repetitive or promotional spam.
  • Avoid fake engagement or misleading advertisements.
  • Do not use bots to manipulate views or followers.

5. Follow the Law

Users must comply with all applicable laws and regulations while using VideoSnap.

6. Reporting Violations

If you find content that violates these guidelines, please report it. Our moderation team will review reported content and take appropriate action.

7. Enforcement

Accounts or content that violate these guidelines may be removed, suspended, or permanently banned from VideoSnap.
