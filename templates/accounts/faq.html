Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about VideoSnap.

1. What is VideoSnap?

VideoSnap is a platform where users can upload, organize, and manage videos securely.

2. Is VideoSnap free?

Yes. Most features are available for free. Premium features may be introduced in the future.

3. Do I need an account?

You can browse public pages without an account, but uploading and managing videos requires registration.

4. Can I upload any video?

You may upload videos that you own or have permission to use. Content that violates our policies may be removed.

5. How do I reset my password?

Click the "Forgot Password" option on the Login page and follow the instructions.

6. How do I contact support?

Visit our Contact page and submit the contact form. Our team will respond as soon as possible.

7. Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard security practices to help protect your account and uploaded content.

8. Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can request account deletion through your profile settings or by contacting support.
