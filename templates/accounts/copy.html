Copyright Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

VideoSnap respects intellectual property rights and expects all users to do the same. This Copyright Policy explains how copyrighted content is handled on our platform.

1. Ownership of Content

Users retain ownership of the videos and content they upload. By uploading content, you confirm that you own the rights or have permission from the copyright owner.

2. Prohibited Content

  • Uploading copyrighted videos without permission.
  • Sharing copyrighted music, movies, or TV shows illegally.
  • Using someone else's work without proper authorization.

3. Copyright Complaints

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without permission, you may submit a copyright complaint with sufficient details so that we can investigate the issue.

4. Removal of Content

VideoSnap may remove or disable access to content that is found to infringe copyright laws or violates this policy.

5. Repeat Infringers

Accounts that repeatedly violate copyright laws may be suspended or permanently removed from the platform.

6. Fair Use

Some content may qualify as fair use under applicable copyright laws. Each case is reviewed individually based on the information provided.

7. Contact

For copyright-related questions or complaints, please contact the VideoSnap support team through our Contact page.
