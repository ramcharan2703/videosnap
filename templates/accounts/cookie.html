Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how VideoSnap uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help remember your preferences and improve website performance.

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • Keep you signed in securely.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Improve website speed and performance.
  • Understand how visitors use VideoSnap.
  • Enhance your overall user experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies – Required for basic website functionality.
  • Performance Cookies – Help us improve website performance.
  • Preference Cookies – Remember your settings and preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies – Collect anonymous usage statistics.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling some cookies may affect certain website features.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some services integrated into VideoSnap may use third-party cookies for analytics, security, or embedded content.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Cookie Policy, please contact the VideoSnap support team.
